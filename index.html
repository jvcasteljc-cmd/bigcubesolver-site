What it does

Most solvers stop at 6×6 or 7×7. This one keeps going to 15×15 - and it runs on modest phones too, from Android 5.0 up.

Big cubes, short solutions

Custom solvers reduce a 15×15 to a 13×13, then 11×11 ... down to a 3×3, so you never wade through thousands of needless moves.

Follow at your pace

Animated 3D moves you can step, rewind, slow down or speed up, in chapters: centres, edges, the final 3×3.

Picture cubes, solved for real

Turned centres are solved too, so every photo or logo comes back whole and upright.

Scan with the camera

Show each face and the colours are read automatically - odd colour schemes are read as they are.

Patterns

Spirals, rings, checkerboards, cubes in cubes on every size - and your own, shared as a link.

Your own designs

Draw on a face or place stickers one by one, and the app finds the moves.

Short solutions, every size
